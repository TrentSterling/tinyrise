Quick reference

Controls

Neighbourhood: choose occupants, mark indoor bays on the floor plan, and fit out the space. The existing shell is free; fit-outs, amenities, and expansion use refundable credits. Earn community grants and host busy days when ready. The old presets remain unrestricted free build.

Click a roof to add a storey, or a wall to extend sideways. Neighbouring cells share façades. Roofs, glass, parapets and industrial details follow the shape. There is no economy or gravity simulation. The Living Edition adds destination-driven people, explicit access links and optional commissions. Use the Live tab for roof/plaza activities and Connect for entrances, stair cores and skybridges.

Lift adds one storey to the connected, flat roof you click. It preserves each cell's material. A 2 × 2 or 3 × 3 brush works in the plane of the selected face. Erasing does not automatically delete the blocks above.

Interior movement uses an abstract floor-and-stair graph. Free camera has no collision or gravity. Browser saving is best effort; JSON is your portable backup.

Development & technology

AI-assisted development under Trent Sterling’s direction, with iterative playtesting and visual review. Building meshes, inhabitants, sounds, and shaders are generated in this project.

The default renderer is embedded WebGL2. No installation, account, or remote assets are needed to play. The optional ?backend=three adapter loads Three.js r180 from a CDN; that path is not part of the validated offline build. Three.js / authors / MIT license.

Neighbour rules build the architecture; this is not a reconstruction of the reference games’ private algorithms. Public reading: Miniopolis public breakdown · Oskar Stålberg’s tile-based towns talk · Maxim Gumin’s WFC reference. WFC was background reading, not a runtime dependency.

Save files use the tinyrise-building and tinyrise-city format IDs. JSON and GLB identify the software creator; they do not assign authorship of your building to someone else.
